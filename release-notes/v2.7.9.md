# Geekify v2.7.9 - Release Notes

**Release Date:** 18 November 2026

### Improvements
- **Add Account icon**: Refined the "Add another account" icon in Accounts sheet to a geometric silhouette with a rounded plus badge.
- **Side sheet appearance**: Smoothed the accounts panel background with a uniform dark veil and subtle header glow.

### Bug Fixes
- **ASCII background color accuracy**: Fixed inverted red and blue channels across both CPU blit and GPU shader paths, restoring authentic creature and landscape colors.
- **Previous track button**: Fixed previous track navigation to smoothly skip to the previous track on rapid double-press and properly wrap around when repeat-all is enabled.
