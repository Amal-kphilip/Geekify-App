# Geekify v2.8.1 - Release Notes

**Release Date:** 20 November 2026

### What's New
- **Double-tap artwork seeking**: Added customizable double-tap left/right seeking on the album artwork across all six expanded player layouts with configurable skip duration and visual feedback.
- **Now Playing to Lyrics shared transition**: Added a dedicated shared-element transition that smoothly morphs album artwork and track details between Now Playing and the full-viewport Lyrics view with predictive-back scrubbing.
- **Sohne Breit typography and lyrics preview**: Added the Sohne Breit font family across the app and lyrics view, along with a live synchronized lyrics preview in Personalization Studio.
- **Show Translated Lyrics toggle**: Added a dedicated toggle to show or hide translated lyrics directly from the lyrics screen and settings, persisted across sessions.

### Improvements
- **Global settings UI redesign**: Standardized settings cards, icon tiles, spacing, responsive text wrapping, and selected-value alignment across Settings and Personalization Studio.
- **Seekbar customization cleanup**: Simplified seekbar styles to Classic Rounded, Wavy, Thin Linear, Thick Capsule, and Wave Glow with automatic preference migration for removed styles.
- **Theme and customization refinements**: Improved Light and Dark Theme contrast, Acrylic and Liquid Glass controls, ASCII background customization, Home section ordering, and compact Navigation ordering.
- **Compiler warning cleanup**: Resolved all Kotlin compiler warnings in the debug build without altering runtime behavior.

### Bug Fixes
- **Duplicate lyrics elimination**: Fixed TTML paragraph-level and span-level translation/romanization parsing and LRC deduplication so translated lines no longer duplicate original lyrics.
- **Lyrics auto-scroll and playback sync**: Fixed active-line visibility on open, automatic scrolling during playback, and seekbar/lyrics synchronization after long pauses.
- **Navigation and predictive-back transitions**: Replaced overlapping NavHost crossfades and default shrinking-card predictive-back transitions with a unified non-overlapping staggered motion spec across all destinations and settings subpages while keeping the ASCII background uninterrupted and preserving the Mini-Player to Now Playing transition.
- **Mini-player customization gating**: Fixed mini-player sub-settings visibility and overlap behavior across docked and inline modes.
