# Geekify v2.7.7 - Release Notes

**Release Date:** 16 November 2026

### What's New
- **Pinned Home header**: Profile, Search and Liked songs buttons now stay fixed at the top while you scroll Home.

### Improvements
- **Smoother background animations**: Offloaded ASCII background simulation to a background thread and added glyph-atlas rendering for improved smoothness on dense scenes like Ocean Waves and Aurora.
- **Playback-state indicators**: Play buttons across Search top results, Home discover cards, and playlist rows now dynamically reflect whether a song is actively playing or paused.

### Bug Fixes
- **Search privacy**: Fixed search history persisting after signing out or switching accounts.
- **Play button toggle**: Fixed play buttons on Search top results and Home daily picks not toggling play/pause when the song is currently loaded.
